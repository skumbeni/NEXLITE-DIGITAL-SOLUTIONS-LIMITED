Why offline-first

Connectivity is a feature, not a given.

Power cuts and patchy data are daily reality for depot operators, farmers, and rural schools alike. Our apps run fully offline — some, like Satellite Depot Tracker, even open with your last-known stock data when there's no signal at all — and sync the moment a connection reappears.

Why mobile money

Billing in the currency people already use.

Airtel Money, MTN MoMo, and Zamtel Kwacha aren't an afterthought — they're how our pricing and payments are built from day one, across depot billing, savings-group contributions, and transport fares alike.

Why we build for the hard cases

If it works here, it works anywhere.

From seasonal agricultural cycles to community-run finance groups, we design for the messiest real conditions — patchy connectivity, informal cash flows, no forgiving IT department. Solve for that, and the software holds up anywhere formal infrastructure hasn't fully caught up to how people actually transact.

Common questions

Before you download.

Are these apps free?

Most apps have a free tier or trial so you can try them before paying anything — check the "Learn More" details on each app above for its exact pricing, since this varies by product and some are still pre-launch. Paid tiers unlock more features and higher usage limits.

Do they work without internet?

Yes — our apps are built offline-first, meaning you can keep working through load shedding or patchy data, and your information syncs to the cloud automatically once you're back online.

How do I install an app?

Tap "Open App" on any product above. Most of our apps run directly in your phone's browser as a web app, with an option to install it to your home screen for quicker access — no app store required.

Can I pay with mobile money?

Yes. Billing is built around Airtel Money, MTN MoMo, and Zamtel Kwacha, since that's how most of our customers already pay for everything else.

Is my data safe?

Yes. Our apps use hardened Firebase security rules to keep your data isolated from other accounts or depots, App Check to block automated abuse, and secure password hashing (PBKDF2 or bcrypt, depending on the app) rather than storing plain-text passwords. We don't sell customer data to third parties.

I need help — who do I contact?

Reach us directly on WhatsApp or email using the links in the footer below. We're a small team and you'll be talking to the people who actually built the app, not a call centre.
