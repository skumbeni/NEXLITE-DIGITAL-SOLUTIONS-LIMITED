Common questions

Before you download.

Are these apps free?

Each app has a free tier or trial so you can try it before paying anything. My Depot Master and SME Accounts Pro both have a free Starter tier, School Pro Ultimate offers a 14-day free trial, and MotoConnect's pricing will be announced at launch. Paid tiers unlock more features and higher usage limits.

Do they work without internet?

Yes — My Depot Master, SME Accounts Pro, and School Pro Ultimate are built offline-first, meaning you can keep working through load shedding or patchy data, and your information syncs to the cloud automatically once you're back online.

How do I install an app?

Tap "Open App" on any product above. Most of our apps run directly in your phone's browser as a web app, with an option to install it to your home screen for quicker access — no app store required.

Can I pay with mobile money?

Yes. Billing is built around Airtel Money, MTN MoMo, and Zamtel Kwacha, since that's how most of our customers already pay for everything else.

Is my data safe?

Your data is stored securely and only accessible to you and the people you choose to give access to within your account. We don't sell customer data to third parties.

I need help — who do I contact?

Reach us directly on WhatsApp or email using the links in the footer below. We're a small team and you'll be talking to the people who actually built the app, not a call centre.
