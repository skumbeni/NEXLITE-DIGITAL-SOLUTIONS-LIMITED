Why generic school software doesn't fit Zambian schools

Most school management platforms are built assuming steady broadband, a dedicated IT administrator, and a single school with no need to isolate data from any other. That's not the reality for most schools in Zambia — many share buildings, staff, or ownership groups running more than one school, with internet that comes and goes depending on the day.

School Pro Ultimate (also known as EduGenius) was built the other way round: offline-first, with proper data isolation for schools that operate as part of a group, so records for one school never bleed into another's.

Frequently asked questions

Can I manage more than one school?

Yes. School Pro Ultimate supports multi-school data isolation, so each school's records stay separate even under one account.

Yes. It's offline-first — keep working with no connection, and everything syncs automatically once you're back online.

Can I track school fees per student?

Yes. Each student's fee balance is tracked individually, so outstanding payments are always clear.
