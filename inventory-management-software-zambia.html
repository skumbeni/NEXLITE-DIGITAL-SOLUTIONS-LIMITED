Why generic inventory apps don't fit Zambian businesses

Most inventory software on the market is built somewhere else, for someone else's problems. It assumes steady broadband, card-based payments, and a single warehouse with one manager. That's not how a depot in Mansa, a wholesaler in Kitwe, or a shop near a rural school actually operates.

Load shedding cuts power mid-shift. Mobile data runs out before the month does. Staff rotate between counting stock, taking payments, and managing customers who buy on credit and pay in instalments. A tool that only works with a live internet connection isn't a tool you can rely on — it's a liability waiting for the next outage.

My Depot Master was built the other way round: offline-first from day one, with cloud sync as the backup, not the requirement.

What My Depot Master actually does

It's a single tool that covers the day-to-day reality of running a depot, shop, or small warehouse — not a bloated system built for a head office you don't have.

Frequently asked questions

Does it work without internet?

Yes. My Depot Master is offline-first — you keep logging stock and sales with no connection at all, and everything syncs to the cloud automatically once you're back online.

Can I use it for a single shop or a full depot?

Both. It scales from a single-till shop up to a multi-staff depot with shift logs, staff records, and full stock history.

Yes — there's a free tier so you can try it with your own stock before committing to a paid plan. Pricing scales with the features and usage you need.

Yes. Billing is built around Airtel Money, MTN MoMo, and Zamtel Kwacha.
