Why chilimba groups fall apart over records, not money

Most chilimba groups don't fail because the money isn't there — they fail because nobody trusts the record-keeping. One person tracks contributions in a notebook that can be lost, disputed, or simply forgotten to update. Rotation order gets fuzzy. Someone misses a payment and it's not clear if they've caught up. By the time it's sorted out, trust between members has already taken a hit.

Chilimba Master exists to remove that friction — a shared, always-current record that every member can check, with clear rules around contributions, fines, and payout order built in from the start.

What Chilimba Master actually does

Everything a chair, treasurer, or ordinary member needs to run a chilimba group without disputes.

Contribution tracking

Log every member's contribution as it comes in, with a running balance visible to the whole group.

Rotation management

Track whose turn it is to receive the payout, with automatic cycle restarts once a round completes.

Fines and penalties

Apply and track fines for late or missed contributions, so the rules are enforced consistently, not by memory.

Role-based permissions

Chairs, treasurers, and members each see and do only what their role allows — no single point of failure.

WhatsApp summaries

Share contribution and rotation summaries straight to the group's WhatsApp, so no one has to ask "where do we stand?"

Works fully offline

Record contributions with no signal — everything syncs automatically once you're back online.

Frequently asked questions

Can every member see the group's records?

Yes — visibility is built around transparency, with permissions set by role so members can see contributions and rotation order without needing to ask the chair.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Chilimba Master is offline-first — record contributions with no connection at all, and everything syncs once you're back online.

Can I apply fines for late payments?

Yes. Fines and penalties for late or missed contributions can be applied and tracked directly in the app.

Does it send updates to WhatsApp?

Yes — contribution and rotation summaries can be shared straight to your group's WhatsApp chat.
