Delete your Shop Inventory & Sales Manager account

App: Shop Inventory & Sales Manager by NEXLITE DIGITAL SOLUTIONS LIMITED.

Option 1 — in the app

  1. Log in as the admin.
  2. Open Settings → Data & Backup → My Account.
  3. Tap Delete My Account, follow the steps, type your company name and enter your admin password.

Tip: use Export My Data first if you want a copy.

Option 2 — request deletion from us

If you cannot log in, send your company name, admin username and the phone number or email used to register by email to support@nexlitedigitalsolutionslimited.digital or WhatsApp/call +260 960 271 128. We verify ownership and delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

Company profile, stock records, staff records and earnings history, financial records and reports, and your cloud backup. This cannot be undone.

What is kept

Subscription payment and licence records are kept only as needed for accounting and legal obligations.
