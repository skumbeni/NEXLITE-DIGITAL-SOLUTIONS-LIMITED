What it does
Features
01 /
🌽
Commodity Tracking
Receive, issue and balance any commodity per named batch. Stock counts, variance reports, transport log and full batch-to-source traceability.
02 /
📦
General Inventory
Manage fertiliser, equipment, supplies and any other item. Expiry tracking, low-stock alerts, credit/debt status and per-item running balances.
03 /
👷
Staff & Payroll
Clock in/out shifts, log work by bag, hour or day. Auto-calculate wages, deductions, payments, leave balances and print credit-card-sized ID cards.
04 /
📊
Financial Reports
Full Profit & Loss, Cash Flow statement and Budget vs Actual — covering both inventory and commodity data consolidated in one view.
05 /
🏢
Contacts & Suppliers
Store suppliers and customers with balances. Link receive records to suppliers and track Supplier Performance with delivery and quality scores.
06 /
📉
Smart Dashboard
Live overview of your entire operation. Customisable widgets. Automatic alerts for variance, low stock, expiry, negative balances and outstanding credit.
07 /
🚛
Transport & Traceability
Log vehicles, drivers, routes, trip costs and notes. Trace any issued batch back to its exact receive record and supplier — full supply chain visibility.
08 /
📒
Books & Spreadsheets
Built-in spreadsheet editor with formulas and document creator with tables. Create, edit and download files directly inside the app — no external tools needed.
09 /
☁️
Cloud Sync & Offline
Works fully without internet. Auto-syncs to Firebase when online. PIN and email account recovery. Per-tab cloud upload and download controls.
10 /
👥
Multi-User & Roles
Admin and staff roles with different access levels. Read-only mode for staff. Configurable edit window, audit trail and 24-hour change protection.
11 /
🔍
Global Search & Export
Instant global search across all records — stock, staff, commodities and contacts. Export any view to PDF or CSV with one tap.
12 /
🔒
Secure & Private
PBKDF2 encrypted passwords, fingerprint login, auto-logout, brute-force protection and Content Security Policy. Your data never leaves your control.
Getting started
In Four Steps
Download & Install
Download the APK directly to your Android phone or open the web app in Chrome. No Play Store required. Tap install and it's on your home screen in seconds.
Set Up Your Company
Enter your company name, location and define your commodities, stock items and staff roles. Takes about 5 minutes. Your 1-month free trial starts immediately.
Start Recording
Log stock received, issued, staff work, payments and inventory. The dashboard updates in real time. Works without internet — syncs to cloud when you're back online.
Subscribe & Keep Going
After your trial, pick a plan from K90/month, pay via Airtel Money or MTN MoMo, and send your Company Key on WhatsApp. Activation is confirmed within 48 hours — or stay on the Free plan, always.
