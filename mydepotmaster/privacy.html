Shop Inventory & Sales Manager — Privacy Policy

Last updated 2 November 2026

1. Who we are

Shop Inventory & Sales Manager is developed and operated by NEXLITE DIGITAL SOLUTIONS LIMITED (Reg. No. 120261050528), trading as Spike Trading and General Dealers ("the Developer"), a PACRA-registered software company based in Zambia. Contact: support@nexlitedigitalsolutionslimited.digital.

2. What data we collect

Only the data you enter into the app: company name, province, district and country; the contact person’s name and phone number given at signup; admin username and hashed password or PIN; recovery email address; stock, sales, purchase and financial records; staff records (names, phone numbers, work logs, earnings, leave and payroll figures you choose to record); optional files you upload (staff profile photos, staff employment contracts as PDF or image, and your depot logo); questions you type into the Depot Assistant; login history, including the IP address seen at sign-in; anonymous app error reports; and device/session identifiers stored on your device. On the website version, subscription payment requests (plan and payment reference) are also stored. The Google Play app does not sell subscriptions or take payment. We do not collect personal data of your end customers beyond what you choose to record.

3. How we use your data

Solely to provide the app's business-management features to you, to activate your subscription and to fix bugs. We do not analyse, profile, sell, rent or share your data with third parties for marketing.

4. Cloud sync and third parties

If you enable cloud sync, your data is stored on Google Firebase (Realtime Database, Authentication and App Check). Transfers use HTTPS. Firebase App Check uses Google reCAPTCHA, which receives device and browser signals under Google’s own privacy policy.

A few features send limited data to other services:

  • Depot Assistant (AI): when you use it, your question and the depot records relevant to it are sent through our server proxy on Cloudflare to Google’s Gemini service to produce an answer. Using the Assistant is optional. Its answers can be wrong, so check them before acting.
  • Sign-in IP lookup: at sign-in the app asks api.ipify.org for your public IP address, which is recorded in your login history.
  • Exchange rates: the app fetches rates from open.er-api.com. No depot data is sent.

Google and Cloudflare run servers in several countries, so data you sync or send to the Assistant may be processed outside Zambia. We do not sell your data or share it for marketing. If you turn cloud sync off, data stays on your device, except Assistant questions and the lookups above.

5. Legal basis (Zambia Data Protection Act No. 3 of 2021)

Our lawful basis is your explicit consent, given when you accept these terms at signup and login. You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account. For operators outside Zambia, processing is based on performance of the contract (providing the software service).

6. Controller and processor

You are the data controller for the business and personal records you enter (including staff records). The Developer acts only as a data processor providing the software. You are responsible for using the app in line with the Data Protection Act 2021 and applicable employment, financial and sector laws.

7. Retention and deletion

Data is kept while your account is active. You can delete your account and all company data yourself in the app (see our deletion page); deletion removes the company profile, stock and financial records, staff records, and cloud backup. Subscription payment and licence records are kept only as long as needed for accounting and legal obligations. You can also request deletion by contacting us; we act within 30 days.

8. Security

Passwords are hashed before storage. Cloud sync uses HTTPS and Firebase authentication tokens. No system is completely secure; use a strong password and do not share login details.

9. Your rights

You may access, correct, erase, restrict or object to processing of your data, and withdraw consent. Contact support@nexlitedigitalsolutionslimited.digital.

10. Children

The app is not for anyone under 18 and we do not knowingly collect data from minors.
