Why most accounting software doesn't fit a Zambian SME

Most accounting platforms are built for businesses with a finance department, a stable internet line, and customers who pay by card on the day of purchase. That's not the reality for most small businesses in Zambia. You're the owner, the cashier, and the bookkeeper — often all in the same hour. Your customers pay in mobile money, sometimes in instalments, sometimes on credit you're trusting them to settle later.

A system that needs constant connectivity or assumes a dedicated accountant just adds friction to a job that's already hard enough. SME Accounts Pro is built the other way round: simple enough to run yourself, offline-first so a bad network day doesn't stop you recording a sale, and built around the Kwacha and mobile money from the ground up.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need accounting experience to use it?

No. SME Accounts Pro is built for business owners doing their own books, not trained accountants — everyday language, no ledger jargon required.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. It's offline-first — record income, expenses, and credit sales with no connection at all, and everything syncs automatically once you're back online.

Can I track customers who buy on credit?

Yes. Each customer gets a running credit balance so you always know who owes what and when it was recorded.

Can I pay with mobile money?

Yes. Billing is built around Airtel Money, MTN MoMo, and Zamtel Kwacha.
