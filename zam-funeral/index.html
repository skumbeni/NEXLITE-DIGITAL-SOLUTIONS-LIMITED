THE PROBLEM

Funeral funds run on trust — and trust breaks when the records don't add up.

Most burial societies and staff welfare funds still track contributions in an exercise book or a phone's Notes app. When a member's family needs a payout, someone has to reconstruct months of payments from memory, screenshots, and mobile money SMS. A dashboard fixes that.

WHO IT'S FOR

Any group where members pay in, and the fund pays out

If contributions and payouts are currently tracked by hand, this replaces the exercise book without changing how your fund actually runs.

QUESTIONS

Common questions

What happens if the chairperson's phone breaks?

Log in with your chairperson email from any device — the fund's data is tied to that account, not to one phone. Also export a backup regularly from Reports.

Does it need internet to work?

No. It works fully offline and syncs in the background once the device is back online.

Can members see each other's contributions?

No. The member portal only shows the contribution history for the phone number that's looked up — nothing about other members.

How is the fund's money handled?

The app doesn't hold or move money. It tracks records only — contributions still go to the fund's own mobile money number, exactly as they do today.

Can we switch chairpersons later?

Yes. Log in with a new email on the home page to create a fresh chairperson account — the fund's records stay exactly as they are.

Is this only for one fund, or can it manage several?

Each installation runs one fund. A committee managing more than one fund would set up a separate instance for each.

What if we stop paying the subscription?

The fund's records stay on the chairperson's device either way — export a backup any time from Reports, regardless of subscription status.
