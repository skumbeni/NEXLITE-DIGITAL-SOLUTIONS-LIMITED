What you get

Everything below is in the current version of the app.

A real till

Barcode scanning, held sales, and thermal receipts through a Bluetooth printer bridge.

Shifts and daily reports

Open and close shifts, with X and Z reports so you see what the cash should be against what was counted.

Voids and refunds

Every void or refund is a new record linked to the original sale, with a reason, so nothing is quietly deleted.

Stock control

Retail and wholesale price levels, expiry dates and batches, and reorder suggestions from your minimum stock.

Profit you can see

Margin reports from your cost prices, plus customer credit and cash summaries.

Staff and roles

Admin, Manager and Staff access, payroll with payslips, and self clock-in with approval.

Works offline

Keep selling through load-shedding. Your records sync to the cloud when you are back online, and a nightly backup runs on our side.

Help built in

A step-by-step User Guide inside the app, an AI Depot Assistant, and exports to CSV and Excel.
