User guide

A quick reference for using Pool. Tap a section to open it.

What is Pool?

Pool helps a cooperative track everyone's produce, sell it together, and split the proceeds fairly — plus manage advances, mobile money payouts, and shared community projects.

There are two kinds of accounts: Admin (usually the treasurer or secretary — can add records) and Member (read-only view of their own and the coop's records).

Getting started

Setting up a coop (admin): Use "Set up your coop" on the landing screen, give it a name, and create your admin sign-in.

Joining as a member: Your admin adds you under Members, then shares a one-time invite link with you. Open it and set up your own sign-in — it only works once.

Every account signs in with its own email and password, kept private to that person.

Members

Admins add members with a name, phone number, and village. Each member's page shows their total contributions, advances, and payout history.

From a member's page, an admin can generate an invite link so that member can sign in and see their own records.

Seasons & contributions

A season is one round of pooling a particular crop (e.g. "Maize — 2026 rainy season"). While it's open, admins log each member's contribution in kilograms and grade.

When the harvest is sold, the admin records the sale (buyer, price, any transport/handling cost) and finalizes it. Finalizing can't be undone — it calculates and locks in every member's share.

Payouts

After a sale is finalized, each member's share appears here as a payout, either due or paid.

An admin can mark a payout paid in cash, or log a mobile money transfer (network, phone, and the reference from the confirmation SMS) for a paper trail.

Marking a payout paid isn't final until the member confirms it — see "Approvals" below.

Advances

An advance is cash or farm inputs given ahead of the harvest, deducted later from that member's payout.

Either side can start one: an admin can give an advance, or a member can request one. Either way, it needs the other side's confirmation before it's recorded (see "Approvals").

Outstanding advances are automatically deducted from that member's next payout(s) until fully repaid.

Projects

A project is a coop-agreed initiative funded separately from harvest pooling — a grinder, storage shed, joint input order, etc. Members can see progress toward the funding target.

Admins record cash contributions toward a project, and mark it completed once the funds are used — completion also needs member confirmation.

Approvals (duo sign-off)

Every movement of funds — a payout, an advance, or a project payout — needs agreement from both sides: whoever didn't start it has to confirm it before anything actually moves.

Open the Approvals tab (the checkmark icon at the top) to see what's waiting on you, what you've sent that's still waiting on the other side, and recent decisions. You can also cancel a request you started, as long as no one has acted on it yet.

Transaction PIN

Confirming any funds movement — finalizing a sale, or approving a payout, advance, or project completion — asks for your transaction PIN, a short PIN separate from your sign-in password.

Set or change it any time from Settings. The first time you confirm a transaction without one set, you'll be asked to choose one on the spot.

Keep it private, and don't share it with anyone — including other members of your coop.

The coop assistant

Tap the chat button to ask questions about your coop's own records — outstanding payouts, unsold seasons, project progress — or get a plain-language summary. It only answers from your coop's data.

Troubleshooting

Forgot your password? Use the reset link on the sign-in screen.

Forgot your transaction PIN? There's currently no self-service reset — contact whoever manages this Pool for your coop.

Offline banner showing? Your changes are saved once your phone reconnects — nothing is lost, but wait for a connection before assuming a record went through.

Invite link not working? Each link works once. Ask your admin to generate a fresh one.

Sold alone

One sack doesn't set the price. It just takes whatever it's offered.

A farmer walks to the roadside with what one season gave them — a few bags, maybe a good year, maybe not. A buyer names a number. There's no room to argue it: down the road, the next farmer will take it if you don't.

This happens the same way in the next village, and the one after that. Everyone selling alone, everyone taking whatever price shows up that day. The real cost isn't just a lower price — it's going through it by yourself.

And even where cooperatives already exist and already sell together, the harder problem is usually what comes after the sale: whose word do you trust for how many kilos you actually brought in back in March? Paper records get lost, memories get fuzzy, and the person keeping the books is doing it by hand on top of everything else they farm.

Together

Thirty-seven small deliveries become one truckload — and one truckload can ask for a fair price.

Pool is built around a simple idea your cooperative already believes in: what one member can't negotiate alone, thirty-seven members can. Every delivery gets logged as it happens. The season's shared total grows in the open, where every member can watch it fill. When it's time to sell, the coop sells once, as one lot — and the price that gets negotiated is one everyone shares in.

Then Pool does the part that used to cause the most arguments: splitting the money back out. Not by guesswork, not by who asked first — by exactly what each person put in, calculated down to the ngwee.

The process

What happens between a delivery and a payout

Deliver & record
Every delivery is written down the day it happens — whose it was, how many kilos, what grade. No end-of-season memory required.
Watch the silo fill
The season's pooled total grows where every member can see it — not a private ledger, a shared one.
Sell once, pay out exactly
One sale, split by each person's true share — no rounding drift, no disputes about who's owed what.

Before you sign in

Questions every member asks first

What if I miss a delivery day, or forget to log one?
Nothing is lost — a delivery just isn't counted until it's recorded. Log it as soon as you can, even the next day; it'll still count toward that season as long as the season is still open.
Can I check my own numbers without asking anyone?
Yes. Every member can open their own history and see every delivery they've ever made — the date, the weight, the grade — not just a single running total.
What happens if two people try to finalize a sale at the same time?
Only the first one goes through. The second is stopped automatically and told the season's already been sold, so a season can never be paid out twice.
Do I need airtime or data to use Pool in the field?
No — entries save on the phone first and sync once there's a connection. A banner lets you know when you're working offline.
Who can start a season or record a sale?
That's set by your cooperative when your account is created — ask your Pool administrator what your account can do.
