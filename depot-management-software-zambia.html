Why running multiple depots gets messy fast

One depot is manageable with a notebook and a good memory. Two or more, and that breaks down quickly. Stock counts live in different books at different sites. Staff move between locations and nobody has a clean record of who was where. The owner ends up visiting each site just to piece together what's actually happening — or worse, finding out weeks later that something didn't add up.

Most depot software assumes a single site, a single manager, and constant internet to keep everything synced live. Satellite Depot Manager was built around the opposite assumption: multiple sites, multiple managers, and connections that come and go — with everything still landing in one place you can actually trust.

What Satellite Depot Manager actually does

Everything needed to run several depots from one account, without needing to physically be at each one.

Multi-depot dashboard

See stock, sales, and activity across every depot you manage in one view, instead of switching between separate records.

Staff transfers between depots

Frequently asked questions

How many depots can I manage with one account?

Satellite Depot Manager is built to scale from a couple of locations up to many, all visible from the same dashboard.

Does each depot need its own internet connection?

No. Each depot works offline independently and syncs to the central account whenever it has a connection.

Can I move staff between depots?

Yes — staff transfers between depots are tracked directly in the system, with a clear record of the change.

Can depot managers see other depots' data?

Only what their role allows. Access is role-based, so depot-level staff see their own site while owners see everything.
