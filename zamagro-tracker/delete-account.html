Delete your ZamAgro Track account

App: ZamAgro Track by NEXLITE DIGITAL SOLUTIONS LIMITED.

Option 1 — in the app (instant)

  1. Log in as the admin (business owner).
  2. Open Settings → Delete Account.
  3. Enter your admin password, type DELETE, and confirm.

Option 2 — request deletion from us

If you cannot log in, send your business name, admin username and the phone number used to subscribe by WhatsApp/Call to 0977 638 790 / 0960 271 128 or email support@nexlitedigitalsolutionslimited.digital. We verify ownership and delete the account within 7 days.

What is deleted

Business account and login, inventory, sales, purchases, expenses, FISP, credit, customers, suppliers and all other records, staff logins, and cloud sync data. Nightly backups containing it expire within 30 days.

What is kept

Subscription payment records (phone number, plan, transaction ID) are kept only as needed for accounting and legal obligations.
