ZamAgro Track — Privacy Policy

Effective 2 November 2026 · Last updated 2 November 2026 · NEXLITE DIGITAL SOLUTIONS LIMITED (Reg. No. 120261050528, Zambia)

This version applies to the ZamAgro Track Android app on Google Play.

NEXLITE DIGITAL SOLUTIONS LIMITED (“we”, “us”) publishes ZamAgro Track, agro-shop management software for businesses in Zambia. This policy covers the ZamAgro Track Android app on Google Play. The app opens the ZamAgro Track web app, so the same data practices apply in both. It explains what we collect, why, who receives it, how long we keep it, and how you can delete it.

1. Who is responsible for your data

For your account, recovery email, feedback and error reports, we decide why and how the data is used. For the records a shop keeps about its own customers, suppliers and farmers (such as names, phone numbers and NRC numbers), the shop decides what to record, and we store and process that data only on the shop’s behalf to run the service. Shops should record only the customer and farmer details they are allowed to collect.

2. Data we collect

  • Account details (required). Business name, your admin username, any staff usernames and roles, and your password. Passwords are hashed (PBKDF2-SHA-256); we never store them in plain text. Sign-in is provided by Google Firebase Authentication.
  • Business records you enter (required to use the app). Inventory, sales, purchases, expenses, FISP claims, credit records, suppliers, customers (names and phone numbers), farmer NRC numbers if you choose to enter them, business settings, and a business logo if you upload one.
  • Licence information. Your free-trial or licence status and its start and end dates. Where a licence was arranged with us outside the app, we also hold the payment reference needed for our accounting.
  • Recovery email (optional). If you add one, we store it so you can reset the owner password with a 6-digit code sent to that address.
  • Feedback (optional). If you submit feedback: your name, optional business or location, the gender option you pick, a star rating and your comment. Once we approve it, it is shown publicly on the app’s landing page. Please do not include phone numbers or other private details.
  • Error reports (automatic). If the app hits an error it sends a small technical report: the error message, a cleaned technical trace, app version, screen name, your role (admin or staff), device or browser type, whether you were online, how many changes were waiting to sync, and your business ID. Reports are scrubbed of email addresses and long numbers and are designed not to include record contents, names, amounts or phone numbers.
  • Data on your device. The app keeps a copy of your records on your device, in the storage of the browser that displays it, so it works offline. It also keeps a queue of changes waiting to sync.
  • Standard technical data. As with any online service, the servers we use see your IP address and basic request details when the app connects.

We do not collect your location, contacts, microphone, SMS or call logs, or your advertising ID. We do not use advertising or analytics services.

3. How we use your data

  • To run the service: sign you in, show your records, sync them across your devices and your staff’s devices, and produce reports, prints and exports.
  • To keep accounts secure, recover owner access, and manage your trial or licence status.
  • To find and fix bugs, using error reports.
  • To show feedback you chose to submit, after approval.
  • To answer your requests and meet our legal and accounting obligations.

We do not sell or rent your data, do not use it for advertising, and do not use it to profile you or make automated decisions about you.

4. Who we share data with

We share data only with the service providers needed to run the app:

  • Google (Firebase Authentication and Realtime Database on Google Cloud) hosts sign-in and your data. The app also loads the Inter font from Google Fonts, so Google sees your IP address and device details when the font loads.
  • Cloudflare runs our account-recovery service, and an email-delivery service sends the recovery verification code on its behalf.
  • Code-delivery networks (cdnjs by Cloudflare, and unpkg) deliver the open-source libraries used to create PDF, Excel and Word exports. They see your IP address when they serve those files.

Other sharing happens only when you choose it or the law requires it:

  • When you tap WhatsApp, Share or Print, the content you chose (for example a receipt, statement or app link) is handed to that app, such as WhatsApp or the RawBT printing app. Those apps have their own privacy policies, and we do not receive that content.
  • Staff accounts you create can see the business records their role allows.
  • Feedback you submit and we approve is public.
  • We may disclose data when required by Zambian law or to protect rights and safety.

We do not sell personal data and do not share it for advertising.

5. Where your data is stored

Your data is stored on Google Cloud infrastructure through Firebase. Google’s data centres can be outside Zambia, including in the United States, so your data may be processed outside Zambia.

6. Security

  • Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Passwords are hashed with PBKDF2-SHA-256 before storage.
  • Each business’s records are stored separately under its own account and protected by database access rules.
  • Staff devices connect through a private Staff Access Link. Anyone who holds it can view that business’s synced records, so keep it private. The owner can regenerate it any time in Settings.
  • We keep nightly backups for up to 30 days for disaster recovery.

No system is perfectly secure. Protect your password, do not share admin credentials, and sign out on shared devices.

7. How long we keep data

  • Your account and business records are kept while your account is active, until you delete it.
  • Error reports are kept only as long as needed to diagnose and fix bugs.
  • Licence and accounting records are kept only as long as needed for accounting and legal obligations.
  • Backups expire within 30 days.

8. Deleting your account and data

In the app: sign in as the admin, open Settings → Delete Account, enter your admin password and type DELETE to confirm.

Without the app: use our account deletion page, or contact us (see Contact below) with your business name and admin username. We may ask you to confirm that you own the account.

Deleted straight away: your business records and settings, your staff accounts and staff access (including the Staff Access Link), your username directory entries, and your sign-in account. The copy on the device you used is also cleared; copies on other devices stay until you clear that app’s or browser’s site data.

What we may keep, and for how long:

  • Backups that contain your data expire within 30 days.
  • Your recovery email, any feedback you submitted, and technical error reports linked to your account are not removed automatically. We delete them within 30 days of your deletion request.
  • Licence and accounting records are kept only as long as accounting and legal obligations require.

Uninstalling the app does not delete your account or your cloud data.

9. Your rights

Under Zambia’s Data Protection Act, 2021 you may ask to access your personal data, have it corrected or erased, object to or restrict how it is used, and receive it in a portable form. You can view and edit your records in the app, download a full backup at any time, and delete your account as described above. You can also contact us about anything else. If your details were entered by a shop (for example as a customer or farmer), please contact that shop first; we will help the shop respond.

If you are unhappy with how your data is handled, you can complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner of Zambia.

10. Children

ZamAgro Track is business software for adults and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

11. Android permissions and device features

The Android app displays our web app through a browser on your device (usually Google Chrome), which stores the local copy; that browser’s own privacy policy applies to the browser itself. The app uses internet access to sign in and sync. If you upload a business logo or restore a backup, Android’s own file or photo picker opens and the app receives only the file you pick. If you print a receipt, the app hands it to the RawBT printing app. The app does not use your location, contacts, calendar, microphone, SMS or call log.

12. Changes to this policy

We will update this page and the date above when this policy changes. The same text is available inside the app from the Privacy Policy link.
