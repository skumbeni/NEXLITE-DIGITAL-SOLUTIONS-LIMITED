ZamAgro Track — Privacy Policy

Effective 1 July 2025 · Last updated 28 October 2026 · NEXLITE DIGITAL SOLUTIONS LIMITED (Reg. No. 120261050528, Zambia)

NEXLITE DIGITAL SOLUTIONS LIMITED ("we", "us") protects the privacy of users of ZamAgro Track. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Data we collect

Only what you enter: business name, admin and staff usernames (passwords are hashed — we never store plain text), inventory, sales, purchases, expenses, FISP claims, credit records and settings. If you choose to enter them, this includes customer names and phone numbers and farmer NRC numbers. We also keep subscription payment requests (phone number, plan, mobile-money transaction ID) and anonymous app error reports (error message, app version, device type, business ID).

2. How we use it

Solely to run the service: show your records, sync your devices, generate reports, activate subscriptions and fix bugs. We do not sell or rent your data and do not share it for marketing.

3. Storage and security

Data is stored in Google Firebase (Realtime Database and Authentication) on Google Cloud. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Passwords are hashed with PBKDF2-SHA-256. Your data is stored under your own account ID and is not accessible to other users. We keep encrypted-in-transit nightly backups for up to 30 days for disaster recovery.

4. Third parties

Google Firebase for authentication and storage. We use no advertising networks and no analytics trackers.

5. Retention and deletion

Your data is kept while your account is active. You can delete your account and all business data in the app (Settings → Delete Account) or by following our deletion page. Deleted data is removed immediately; backups containing it expire within 30 days. Subscription payment records are kept only as long as needed for accounting and legal obligations.

6. Your rights

You may access, correct or delete your data at any time (in-app backup/export and deletion tools, or contact us).

7. Children

ZamAgro Track is business software and is not directed at children.

8. Changes

We will update this page and the date above when this policy changes.
