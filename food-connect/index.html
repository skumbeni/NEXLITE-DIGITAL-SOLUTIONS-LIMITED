How it works

An independent ordering, purchasing and delivery service, town by town.

Pick your town and restaurant

Choose your town, then browse local menus and fill one basket per order, from one restaurant at a time.

See the total first

Food, service fee and delivery fee are shown before you send your order.

Delivery by distance

Delivery is priced by how far you are from the restaurant: 0 to 3 km, 3 to 6 km, or 6 to 10 km.

Payment first

You pay us by MTN Mobile Money, Airtel Money, or a deposit we confirm with you. We buy your food only after payment is confirmed.

Order on WhatsApp

Your order request opens in WhatsApp with the items and delivery details ready to send. We reply with payment instructions.

Your own account

Sign in with your email and password. Your phone number is saved with your account so we can reach you about your order.

What it costs

You pay the restaurant price for your food, plus our service and delivery fees.

Food

The restaurant's price, confirmed with you before you pay

Service fee

10% of your food total, minimum K10

Delivery

K20 for 0 to 3 km, K30 for 3 to 6 km, K45 for 6 to 10 km

Minimum order

K50 of food per order

The shop shows your exact total before you send your order. If a price or item changes, we ask for your approval before you pay or before we buy.

Questions

Do I pay before you buy my food?

Yes. We confirm the final price and availability with you, then buy your food once your payment is confirmed.

Which restaurants can I order from?

The restaurants listed in the shop. We are an independent service and are not affiliated with or endorsed by the restaurants.

Can I order from two restaurants at once?

Not in one order. Each order is from one restaurant, which keeps buying and delivery simple. You can place a second order for another restaurant.

Where do you deliver?

In the towns listed in the shop. Pick your town when you open it and you will see restaurants for that town. Delivery is up to 10 km from the restaurant you order from.
