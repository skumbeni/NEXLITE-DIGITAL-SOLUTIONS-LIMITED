Delete your account

School Pro Ultimate · NEXLITE Digital Solutions Limited · Privacy Policy · Back to app

In short. Email us from the address on your account and ask us to delete it. We will confirm by email once it is done.

How to ask

  1. Write to support@nexlitedigitalsolutionslimited.digital from the email address used on the account, with the subject "Delete my School Pro account". You can also message +260 977 638 790 on WhatsApp.
  2. Tell us your name, your school's name, and whether you want to delete only your login or the whole school's data (School Administrators only).
  3. We may reply to check it is really you. We will never ask for your password or PIN.

What is deleted

  • Your login (staff or parent): your account, name, email address and the links between your login and the school's learners or classes.
  • A whole school (requested by its Administrator): the school's learners, results, attendance, fees, payroll, timetables, notices, files and all of its staff and parent logins.
  • A learner's record: ask the School first, because the School owns its records. If the School cannot help, write to us.

What may be kept

We may keep a record that a deletion was requested, and anything the law requires us to keep. Copies in routine backups are removed as the backups expire. Records the School has already exported or shared (for example report PDFs sent by WhatsApp) are outside our control.

How long it takes

We aim to complete deletion within 30 days of confirming your request.
