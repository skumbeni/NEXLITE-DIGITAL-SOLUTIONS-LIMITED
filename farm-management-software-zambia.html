Why farm records fall apart in Zambia

Farming and agro-dealing don't happen near reliable internet. Records get kept in exercise books at the field or in the store, updated when there's time, which is rarely often enough. By harvest or sale season, it's hard to reconstruct exactly what was planted, what livestock came and went, or what was actually sold and to whom.

Most farm management software assumes constant connectivity and a desk to sit at — neither of which matches life on a farm or at an agro-dealer's counter. ZamAgro Track is built around the field, not the office: offline-first, with records that sync automatically the moment you're back in range.

What ZamAgro Track actually does

A practical record-keeping system for farm and agribusiness operations, built for real conditions.

Crop tracking

Record planting, inputs, and harvest by crop and season, without relying on memory or a field notebook.

Livestock records

Track livestock numbers, movements, and health events in one running record per animal group.

Works fully offline

Log activity from the field with no signal at all — everything syncs once you're back online.

Sales tracking

Record every sale of produce or livestock, with a running picture of what's actually moved and for how much.

Mobile money billing

Built around Airtel Money, MTN MoMo, and Zamtel Kwacha for how transactions actually happen.

Per-business data isolation

Each farm or agro-dealer's records stay separate and secure, accessible only to their own account.
